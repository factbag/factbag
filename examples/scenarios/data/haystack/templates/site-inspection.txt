SITE INSPECTION REPORT — {{companyCity}} FACILITY
Inspection date: {{memoDate}}
Inspector: independent engineer instructed by the board

SCOPE

This report covers the annual inspection of the {{companyCity}} facility:
process equipment, pressure systems, fire detection, and the works in progress
for the {{projectName}} programme. It does not cover the two smaller sites,
which are inspected on an alternating cycle.

PROCESS EQUIPMENT

The main production line was examined under load. Vibration readings on the
number three drive were above the manufacturer's recommended band and should
be investigated before the next shutdown. The condition monitoring installed
during the year is working as intended and the data is being retained.

Guarding on the packing line has been replaced since the previous inspection
and now meets the applicable standard. Two interlocks were found taped open on
the day of the visit; the site manager removed the tape during the inspection
and has since issued a toolbox briefing.

PRESSURE SYSTEMS

All twelve vessels carry current written schemes of examination. The nitrogen
receiver is due for internal examination before {{facilityMaturity}} and the
site has booked the contractor. Relief valve test certificates were produced
for eleven of the twelve vessels; the missing certificate relates to a valve
replaced in the period and the certificate has since been supplied.

FIRE AND EMERGENCY

Detection coverage in the finished goods warehouse remains thin following the
racking changes. The inspector recommends a detection survey before the racking
is extended further. Evacuation drill records show two drills in the period,
with muster times of 4 minutes 10 seconds and 3 minutes 52 seconds.

{{projectName}} WORKS

Civil works are complete and the mechanical installation is approximately 70%
finished. Temporary power distribution is well managed. The inspector notes
that the permit-to-work system is being applied to contractor activity, and
that the contractor's own supervision is present on site each shift.

Capital expenditure committed to the programme stands at {{capitalExpenditure}},
of which {{capitalSpent}} has been spent. Completion remains scheduled for the
period ending {{periodEnd}}.

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Investigate the number three drive vibration readings.
2. Commission a detection survey for the warehouse racking.
3. Review the interlock defeat procedure with the packing line crews.
4. Retain the relief valve certificate pack in a single location.

This report is issued to the board of {{companyName}} and to the site manager.
